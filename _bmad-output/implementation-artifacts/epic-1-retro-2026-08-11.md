## What Went Well

- **The review-patch count fell every single story (12→10→8→4→1).** Not just fewer bugs — Story 1.5 explicitly states every one of Story 1.3's code-review fixes was "baked in from the start rather than left for review to catch." The team was learning from its own review feedback within the epic, not just across epics.
- **The Architecture Spine stayed honest under real implementation pressure.** Three times a literal architecture rule collided with a genuine implementation need (composition root needing `OrderFlow.DAL` visibility, Form-launching needing an AD-3 exception, concurrency handling needing an AD-9 addition). Each time, the spine was amended and the amendment documented in the story file — not silently violated, not hacked around.
- **Environment constraints were disclosed immediately, not discovered late.** Story 1.1 hit "no `Microsoft.WindowsDesktop.App` on macOS" on the very first story and established the `UNVERIFIED-ENVIRONMENT` labeling convention right there. That label was used consistently through Epic 3, and the underlying gap was eventually closed for real via CI (windows-latest) — see the 2026-08-11 follow-up session, well after this epic shipped.
- **Code review caught real bugs, not just cosmetic issues.** Both defects patched in review (1.2's ordering bug, 1.5's range-clamp crash) were genuine behavioral bugs a user could hit — validates that running full code review on every story, not spot-checking, was worth the overhead.

## Growth Areas

- **Un-fixed debt propagated alongside fixed debt.** Story 1.5 baked in Story 1.3's *code-review fixes* from the start, but also replicated Story 1.3's *un-fixed, deferred* items verbatim: no busy/loading indicator, no empty-state messaging, and `editButton.Enabled` recomputation logic triplicated across three places in both stories. Copying a known-good pattern also copies its known gaps unless someone deliberately breaks the copy.
- **Story 1.2's validate-before-fetch bug is a sequencing bug that's easy to reintroduce anywhere CRUD update logic gets duplicated** (Winston's flag) — worth a deliberate check in any future story that adds another `UpdateAsync`-style method, though Epic 1 was the only place this recurring risk was live to check against.
